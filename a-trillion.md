# Video 3: How big is a trillion?

Sources for the *Actually, No* Short **"How big is a trillion?"**. Each claim in the script is listed with its primary source, the quoted wording, and the short credit shown on screen.

Checked 2026-10-25. Neutral wording: numbers only, no praise or criticism. Every calculation is in `../trillion/tools/check_math.py` (run it to reproduce).

## 1. "One man is now worth a trillion dollars" (0–3 s)
- **Forbes real-time profile** (forbes.com/profile/elon-musk, read 2026-10-25): **$922.9B**, "Reflects change since 5 pm ET of prior trading day". Holdings: SpaceX "38% of the company (including options)"; Tesla "nearly 11%"; SpaceX "acquired xAI in February".
- **Forbes, 12 June 2026**, "SpaceX Shares Close Up 19% After Historic IPO Makes Musk World's First Trillionaire": about $1.1T at the close of SpaceX's first trading day. Forbes later (profile, 14 Oct 2026) says he "briefly" became a trillionaire again.
- **Bloomberg Billionaires Index:** reported at $1.02T at the 7 Oct 2026 close (via secondary coverage; Bloomberg's own page was not opened from here).
- **Check:** on Forbes today he is **below** $1T, so "is now worth a trillion" is not true on 25 Oct 2026. See the open question.

## 2. Seconds (3–15 s): computed
- 1,000,000 s = **11.57 days** (script "11 days": rounding down; safer "over 11 days").
- 1,000,000,000 s = **31.69 years** (script "31 years").
- 1,000,000,000,000 s = **31,689 years** (script "31,700 years").
- Great Pyramid of Giza completed c. **2560 BCE**, about **4,585 years** ago: a trillion seconds reaches about 7× further back.

## 3. "$1M every day … 2,700 years" (15–25 s): computed
- $1,000,000,000,000 ÷ $1,000,000 per day = 1,000,000 days = **2,738 years** (script "2,700 years": "more than 2,700").

## 4. "Seven Warren Buffetts, or 41 years of NASA's budget" (25–33 s)
- **Buffett:** Forbes profile **$154B** (24 Oct 2026). $1T ÷ $154B = **6.49**; Musk's Forbes $922.9B ÷ $154B = **5.99**. "Seven" does not hold today.
- **NASA:** FY2026 enacted appropriation **$24.44B** (Commerce–Justice–Science Appropriations Act, 2026, P.L. 119-74; Senate Commerce Committee release; CRS R43419). $1T ÷ $24.44B = **40.9 years** ✓ ("41 years" holds for a trillion, not for $922.9B = 37.8 years).

## 5. Twist (33–45 s)
- "Almost all of it is shares in SpaceX and Tesla": ✓ per the Forbes holdings above.
- "If he tried to sell, the price would crash before he got close": a reasonable market-impact argument, but a prediction, not a sourced fact. Suggested wording: "selling that much at once would likely push the price down long before he got close."
